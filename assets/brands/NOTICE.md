# Brand Assets

These unchanged images come from the official sites recorded in `sources.json`.
They identify the selected merchant; they do not indicate endorsement or an
integration with the merchant's payment, membership or balance systems.

Brand names, marks and supplied artwork remain the property of their respective
owners. These assets are not licensed under the repository's source-code licence.
Source pages are provenance, not blanket redistribution licences. Preserve the
original form and proportions; Mega's published BI guidance expressly requests
this. Recheck the applicable brand guidance before a new distribution or use.

The picker uses local copies, with `contain` sizing, and falls back to text if an
image is unavailable. Olive Young and Coffee Bean currently use neutral text
tiles. The two Mammoth menu segments share the official site's icon; they retain
separate IDs and menus. Website icons are recorded as identity assets, not as
newly created or independently certified brand artwork.
